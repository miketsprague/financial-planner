Your UK long-term-residence milestone

  • Worldwide US filing obligations

    US citizens and green card holders generally must file a US federal tax return on worldwide income, wherever they live, alongside any UK filing obligations.

    as of 9 August 2026

    • IRS — US citizens and resident aliens abroad
    • GOV.UK — Tax on foreign income and UK residence

  • FBAR and FATCA reporting

    Foreign financial accounts above reporting thresholds may need to be disclosed via FinCEN's FBAR (FinCEN Form 114) and/or IRS Form 8938 under FATCA. The two regimes have different thresholds and forms.

    as of 9 August 2026

    • FinCEN — Report of Foreign Bank and Financial Accounts
    • IRS — Form 8938 and FBAR comparison

  • PFIC — Passive Foreign Investment Companies

    Many non-US-domiciled pooled funds (including many UK funds and ISAs) can be treated as PFICs for US tax purposes, with complex and often unfavourable reporting and taxation. This application never decides whether a specific holding is a PFIC.

    as of 9 August 2026

    • IRS — United Kingdom tax treaty documents

  • UK/US pension treatment

    The US–UK tax treaty addresses some aspects of cross-border pension treatment, but rules differ by pension type and can require specific elections or disclosures.

    as of 9 August 2026

    • IRS — United Kingdom tax treaty documents
    • GOV.UK — Pension scheme rates and allowances

  • Social Security totalisation

    The US–UK social security agreement can help coordinate contribution history between the two systems so that periods of work in each country can count toward benefit eligibility.

    as of 9 August 2026

    • SSA — US–UK social security agreement
    • GOV.UK — The new State Pension

  • UK inheritance tax: long-term-residence milestone

    Since 6 April 2025, UK inheritance tax exposure on worldwide assets can be triggered by having been UK-resident for at least 10 of the previous 20 tax years, replacing the previous domicile-based test.

    as of 9 August 2026

    • GOV.UK — Tax on foreign income and UK residence
